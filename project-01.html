Overview

A recipe-recommendation app concept for people who want quick, easy recipes using only the ingredients they already have at home. The work covers the full UX/UI process: understanding how users discover recipes, designing the experience in Figma, and validating it with usability testing.

What I did

  • Conducted user research to understand how users discover recipes based on available ingredients.
  • Designed user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes in Figma.
  • Conducted usability testing to validate the proposed experience and identify improvements.

Research findings

  • 69.2% of respondents said they "sometimes" find themselves missing ingredients when cooking.
  • Lack of time (61.5%) and not knowing what to cook (38.5%) were the top cooking challenges.
  • 92.3% wanted a feature to flag allergies or chronic conditions for personalized recipes.
  • 69.2% said they'd most likely access a recipe app from a smartphone.

Design process

Paper sketches (low-fidelity) established the homepage, login, and ingredients-input flow. These were digitized into grayscale mid-fidelity wireframes, then into a full-color high-fidelity prototype with recipe cards, ratings, reviews, and a favorites system — all connected through a mapped user flow from onboarding to recipe detail.

Usability testing results

Tested with a 21-year-old and a 55-year-old user. The younger user reached the ingredients page in 15 seconds and the recipes page in 18 seconds with generally positive feedback. The older user took longer (20s and 28s respectively) and had more difficulty with icons and shortcuts — leading to recommendations for clearer navigation, quick tips, and added back-navigation arrows.
