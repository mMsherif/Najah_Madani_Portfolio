Overview

A Figma UI/UX design concept for coordinating crew transportation operations. The screens explore how coordinators can review transport schedules, users, shifts, reports and trip activity, while drivers and crew members receive role-specific trip information and notifications.

Design highlights

  • Administrative dashboard with transport and fleet summaries, trip statistics and recent activity.
  • Interfaces for managing transport schedules, shifts and user records.
  • Reporting views for monitoring trips and operational status.
  • Dedicated dashboard concepts for drivers and crew members, including relevant updates.
  • Early grayscale wireframes and more refined green-themed screens showing the visual design progression.

Design focus

Clear information hierarchy, readable status indicators and intuitive navigation for different user roles. These are Figma design screens, not a claim of a deployed production system.
