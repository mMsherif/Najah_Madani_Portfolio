Overview

Student Shelf is a UI/UX concept for an online bookstore serving students and general readers. The screens organize books by college and discipline and show how visitors could browse collections, explore book details, and interact with a shopping interface.

Design highlights

  • Category-based navigation for medicine, science and engineering, Shari'ah and administration, and humanities and education.
  • Bestseller and sale sections with book covers, prices, and Add to Cart controls.
  • A book detail screen with title, author, publication information, quantity selection, ratings, reviews, and similar books.
  • Welcome page variations highlighting curated collections, promotional offers, rewards, and newsletter signup.

Design focus

Organize a varied book catalog into easy-to-browse sections and make pricing and product information clear. These are interface mockups supplied for the portfolio, not a claim that an e-commerce site is deployed.
