Overview

A voice user interface concept for ordering at Barn's Coffee in Makkah. The design explores hands-free ordering for a busy drive-thru, helping customers customize drinks, confirm orders, and recover from misunderstood speech in noisy environments.

Interaction design

  • Designed voice interactions for five café products, including drink customization, milk options, sweetness levels, and add-ons.
  • Defined explicit confirmations for customized items and a final summary before an order is submitted.
  • Mapped dialogue flows for speech recognition errors, unclear selections, and order corrections.
  • Proposed a drive-thru kiosk and a voice-enabled mobile ordering experience.

UX focus

Improve order clarity, reduce miscommunication, and support accessible, low-effort interaction when users cannot rely on touch screens.
